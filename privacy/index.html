Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

1 Introduction

WeanAtlas respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is handled when a parent or caregiver uses the WeanAtlas mobile app.

WeanAtlas is designed to be local-first. Most baby profile, feeding, preference, grocery, growth, and progress data remains on your device.

2 Information You Provide

  • Baby profile: Name, date of birth, gender, feeding type, notes, and an optional profile photo
  • Feeding information: Allergies, dietary preferences, solids start date, feeding progress, and meal feedback
  • App activity: Growth entries, completed meals, saved foods, custom meals, grocery items, and planner selections
  • Preferences: Region, language, measurement units, and other app settings

This information personalizes meal suggestions, food recommendations, and other app features.

3 Local Data Storage

Baby profile and feeding records are primarily stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server that intentionally stores your baby's complete profile, feeding history, allergies, growth records, or profile photo.

Local information may be lost if you delete the app, reset its data, lose your device, or move to another device without an operating-system backup.

4 Subscriptions and Payments

Apple App Store or Google Play processes subscription payments. RevenueCat helps WeanAtlas display products and verify premium access.

These providers may process an app-specific customer identifier, product and transaction identifiers, purchase and expiration dates, subscription status, country, currency, localized price, offer status, and limited purchase diagnostics.

WeanAtlas may offer weekly and annual subscriptions. Prices and availability vary by country and store. WeanAtlas does not operate its own free trial. Any introductory offer shown at checkout is controlled by the applicable app store.

WeanAtlas does not receive or store your full payment-card details. Baby profile, allergy, growth, meal, and photo data is not intentionally sent to RevenueCat for subscription verification.

5 Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information or your child's data.

  • RevenueCat: Subscription product display, purchase verification, and restoration
  • Apple App Store / Google Play: Payments, subscriptions, distribution, and fraud prevention
  • Expo/EAS: App building, delivery, and updates

Limited information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or when you direct us to do so.

6 Device Permissions

If you select a baby profile photo, WeanAtlas requests photo-library access. The selected photo is stored with the app's local data.

Location access may be requested only when a feature needs it to suggest geographically appropriate foods or regions. You can manage permissions in your device settings.

WeanAtlas does not currently request notification permission, send push notifications, or schedule local meal reminders.

7 Children's Privacy

WeanAtlas is intended for parents and caregivers, not for direct use by children. Information about a baby or child is entered by an adult and is primarily stored locally on that adult's device.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

8 Data Retention and Deletion

You can erase local app data using the reset or deletion options in the app, or by deleting WeanAtlas from your device.

Deleting or resetting WeanAtlas does not cancel a subscription. Subscriptions must be canceled through Apple App Store or Google Play. Store and RevenueCat transaction records may be retained for billing, fraud prevention, legal compliance, and purchase restoration.

9 Security and Policy Changes

We use reasonable safeguards for information handled by the app, but no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above will change when an updated version is published.

10 Contact Us

For privacy questions or data requests, contact devmile45@gmail.com.
